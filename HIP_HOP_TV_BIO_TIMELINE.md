# HIP HOP TV™ — Official Bio & Timeline

**Founded by Fady Atalla | Sydney, Australia**

Hip Hop TV™ is Australia's longest-running urban music program, launched in 2005 and now streaming 24/7 as the country's first dedicated hip hop channel.

---

## Timeline of Key Events

### 2005 — The Launch
- **February 2005:** Hip Hop TV™ debuts on TVS Sydney community television, Channel 31, during the station's pre-launch trials.
- Founded by Fady Atalla as one of the first urban music programs of its kind in Australia.
- Broadcast on a sponsor-based, not-for-profit community channel with limited reach — but a national mission.

### 2000s — Building the Foundation
- Early seasons feature live performances, interviews, and cyphers from the Sydney hip hop scene.
- Hip Hop TV™ becomes a platform for emerging Australian talent alongside international guests.

### 2007 — MTV Recognition
- **2007:** Sharni Jazzie appears at the MTV Awards, featured in Hip Hop TV™ Season 3 content.

### 2010s — Growth & Expansion
- The program expands its reach through YouTube and digital platforms.
- Hip Hop TV™ tours with major international acts, including **Outlaws** and **Bone Thugs-n-Harmony**.
- **2014:** ARIA Awards red carpet coverage and teaser content produced by Hip Hop TV™.
- The show establishes itself as the go-to voice for Australian hip hop news, gigs, and culture.

### 2021–2024 — The Hiatus
- After roughly two decades on air, Hip Hop TV™ takes a four-year break from regular production.

### 2025 — The 25th Anniversary & Relaunch
- **2025:** Hip Hop TV™ celebrates its **25th anniversary** (marking 20 years since the 2005 launch).
- Relaunch after the four-year hiatus with new weekly episodes.
- Launch of Australia's first **24/7 hip hop streaming channel**, broadcasting live on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, and Twitch.
- New content pillars: daily Australian hip hop news, gig guides with live ticket links, reviews, and international coverage.
- Production upgraded with a **JVC GMV 700e broadcast camera**, **DJI Mini 3 Pro drones**, and a crew of camera operators.
- Youth outreach program launched — giving young Sydney artists exposure through live-streamed cyphers and competitions.
- Daily newsletters covering Australian, US, and UK scenes (Drake, Cardi B, Kendrick Lamar, ONEFOUR, and more).
- Website rebuilt at hiphoptv.com.au with a 3-column news layout, RSS feeds, and SEO optimization.
- Promotional campaign: eight-second teasers, blimp and plane banners over Sydney Harbour Bridge and Opera House reading "HIP HOP TV STREAMING 24/7."

### 2026 — Momentum Continues
- New Season 3 episodes in production, including rough cuts and look-backs.
- Continued daily news coverage of Kendrick Lamar's Australian tours, AI music, and the global scene.
- Anthem and theme song released.
- Weekly sneak peeks and new episode drops building toward the biggest year yet.
